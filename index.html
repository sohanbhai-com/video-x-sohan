About Us

Welcome to BIJOY TOPUP. We're a digital top-up and voucher platform built for gamers who don't want to wait around for their diamonds, UC, or in-game currency to arrive. Our goal is simple: make buying game credits fast, safe, and hassle-free.

What We Do

We connect players directly to instant top-ups for their favorite games and platforms — from battle royale UC and diamonds to shell codes and gift vouchers. Orders are processed automatically wherever possible, so most top-ups arrive within seconds of payment confirmation.

Why Choose Us

  • Instant, automated delivery for most products.
  • Secure payments with balance, due-credit, and verified transaction options.
  • Real customer support through available WhatsApp, Telegram, and Messenger channels.
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

Our Commitment

We take account security and order accuracy seriously. If something goes wrong with an order, our support team is ready to help resolve it quickly.

Please reach out through our Support page.

Refund & Return Policy

This policy explains refunds and returns for digital products purchased from BIJOY TOPUP.

1. Digital Products

Top-ups, vouchers, and game credits are digital products. Once successfully delivered to the submitted account or player ID, they cannot normally be returned or refunded.

2. Failed or Cancelled Orders

If an order fails or is cancelled before delivery, the eligible amount will normally be returned to your site wallet or original payment method, depending on the payment process used.

3. Incorrect Information

We cannot refund a successfully delivered order when an incorrect player ID, server, region, or account detail was supplied by the customer.

4. Duplicate or Incorrect Delivery

If you believe an order was duplicated, charged incorrectly, or delivered incorrectly, contact support with the order ID and payment details so the issue can be reviewed.

5. Processing Time

Approved refunds may require several business days depending on the payment provider. Wallet refunds are usually visible sooner.

Delete Account Policy

How to request account deletion on BIJOY TOPUP, and what happens to your data.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion through the support channels listed on our Support page. Include the email, phone number, or linked Telegram account needed to identify your account.

2. What Gets Deleted

After verification, we remove your profile information and disconnect linked accounts. You will no longer be able to sign in after deletion.

3. What We Keep, and Why

Completed order and transaction records may be retained for a limited period where needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, disputes, or other legal obligations.

4. Wallet Balance & Pending Orders

Resolve pending orders and any wallet balance before requesting deletion. Ask support for help before deletion if needed.

5. Processing Time

Requests are normally processed within a few business days. We may ask you to verify account ownership first.
